## Internal-link types
Every editorial link should have a purpose.

- Prerequisite — concept the reader should understand first.
- Deep Dive — expands a component of the current concept.
- Application — shows the concept in underwriting/property analysis.
- Tool — lets the reader calculate or model the concept.

Avoid random “related posts” links.

## End-of-article navigation
Where useful, use:
- Previous concept
- Next concept
- Apply it
- Calculate it

## Glossary linking
Link glossary definitions selectively, normally on the first useful occurrence. Do not link every repeated mention.

## Editorial principle
One topic does not automatically equal one article. A taxonomy node may be a hub, guide, deep dive, glossary entry, calculator, checklist, modeling lesson, reference, comparison, case study or framework. Avoid thin programmatic SEO.
