# ADR 0003: Require authoritative sources and provenance

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-30

## Context

Institutional and program-specific CRE facts can be consequential, nuanced, and time-sensitive. Readers and reviewers need to know where claims came from and when they were checked.

## Decision

Prefer current public authoritative sources for factual claims and retain source provenance in content metadata. Distinguish source categories and authority so reviewers can judge fitness for a claim.

## Consequences

Future publishing workflows must support multiple source records, review dates, and source verification. Provenance improves auditability but does not replace practitioner interpretation or editorial review.
