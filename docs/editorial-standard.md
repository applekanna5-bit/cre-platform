# Human Editorial Standard

## Purpose

Published material must read as though it was written and reviewed by knowledgeable commercial real estate practitioners, not generated at scale. It should help a reader understand both the mechanics of a concept and its significance in actual CRE analysis.

AI may assist with research, organization, drafting, and editing. Publication quality still requires human editorial review against this standard; AI output is never presumed publishable or authoritative.

## Writing and analysis

- Use natural variation in sentence length, paragraph structure, and explanation. Do not force every subject into the same article template.
- Add practitioner interpretation where it clarifies how a concept affects underwriting, lending, valuation, diligence, or investment decisions.
- Use concrete, original examples when they improve understanding. Clearly identify assumptions and do not present illustrative numbers as market facts.
- Separate sourced factual statements from editorial interpretation. Signal analysis, judgment, and inference plainly.
- Explain why a concept matters and how it may influence real CRE analysis, not merely what its definition is.
- Acknowledge legitimate uncertainty, jurisdictional differences, changing program rules, and variation among firms or practitioners.
- Maintain professional, precise, and readable language for the intended audience.

## Integrity and originality

- Do not invent personal experience, transactions, conversations, results, or anecdotes.
- Do not imply that an author or the platform personally underwrote, financed, acquired, or managed a transaction unless that claim is true, authorized, and appropriately documented.
- Avoid generic AI-style introductions and conclusions, filler, repetitive summaries, and unnecessary restatement.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, mass-produced SEO content, and superficial variations of the same page.
- Preserve the meaning, limits, dates, and context of every source. Do not stretch a citation beyond what it supports.
- Produce original synthesis that connects reliable facts with useful analysis. Do not substitute superficial paraphrasing for understanding.

## Publication review

Before publication, an editor must confirm that the work meets this standard, distinguishes fact from interpretation, retains source fidelity, and has received the level of CRE and source review appropriate to its claims.
