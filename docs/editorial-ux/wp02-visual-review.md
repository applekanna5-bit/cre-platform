# WP02 final visual and editorial review

**Recommendation: Approve with Minor Changes.** The publication has a recognizable identity, clear entry points and a useful analytical example. The refinements below concern wayfinding, explanation and scroll length. No Critical or Major issue was observed within the evidence reviewed.

Reviewed `feature/editorial-ux-wp02` at `0ff5dac`. Evidence includes the full desktop and mobile homepage captures, both hero crops, the open mobile menu, the desktop lifecycle page and the mobile specialization page. The seven files in [.worktrees/editorial-wp02-screenshots.zip](../../../editorial-wp02-screenshots.zip) match the loose PNGs by SHA-256. ## What works well

- **Editorial identity.** The paper surfaces, navy text, serif masthead and limited copper rules make the site feel like a publication. The hero explains the subject and gives readers a next step. It needs neither imagery nor a new visual system.
- **Discovery.** Desktop search and the six primary links are easy to find. The [open mobile menu](../../../editorial-wp02-screenshots/mobile-navigation.png) shows all six destinations and a visible focus ring. The desktop lifecycle reads left to right in numbered order. On mobile the stages stack without losing sequence. The numbers explain the process.
- **Reading flow.** A newcomer can start from property income, read the gross-to-effective-income guide and move into the rent roll, trailing 12-month statement or revenue analysis. An experienced reader can go directly to Underwriting, use search or open the Library. Featured Reading offers a concrete reason to read each article. Long titles wrap and stay legible in both captures. Keep the reviewed titles.
- **Editorial boundaries.** The [desktop lifecycle](../../../editorial-wp02-screenshots/desktop-loan-lifecycle.png) places five published readings in Stage 3 and three in Stage 4, with plain-text planned coverage where appropriate. [Mobile specializations](../../../editorial-wp02-screenshots/mobile-specializations.png) provides working in-page destinations. Secondary markets sit under Financing & Markets, with a contextual link from the lifecycle. Servicing has a substantive scope statement and an honest planned note. It has no dummy article links.
- **Model and footer.** The desktop preview fits within a short section and uses one results panel. The mobile input is labelled in USD, the full-dollar outputs fit in the capture, and the reset and full-model links are visible. The full model’s stronger visual treatment makes sense for an interactive tool. Footer groups are distinct on desktop and stack legibly on mobile. Retain their trust links and current touch-target sizing.

The review found no generic marketing promises, fabricated practitioner language or image-heavy template pattern. The repeated rules in the lifecycle and Featured Reading act as list breaks. There is little benefit in replacing them with cards or decoration. “Open the ledger” is the clearest instance where a branded phrase obscures a useful action. “Gross-to-effective-income guide” is also a dense label for a beginner. A plain action such as “Read how vacancy and losses affect income” would make the destination clearer without changing the article title or URL.

## Technical assessment

`HomepageUnderwriting` owns a single draft and passes it and its setter to `UnderwritingModel`. Both views use `parseDraft` and `calculateIllustration` from the same model module. Both reset to the same initial assumptions. The preview’s full-model link is a fragment on the same page. This is supported by the prior WP02 browser check, which showed $18,654,545 at baseline, $18,666,982 at $1,501 monthly rent, and restoration on reset. The fixed 100-unit scenario, formatting and approved financial logic should stay intact.

Both compact outputs have accessible labels. Their live updates are suppressed locally, while the full model has a polite status region that receives shared updates. The source shows that an announcement mechanism exists. A screen-reader check during a follow-up should establish whether it is easy to understand from the compact input and whether small edits produce an appropriate number of announcements. The focus ring visible in the open mobile menu supports the prior keyboard evidence; it does not establish screen-reader behavior or contrast compliance across every state.

Prioritize the publication-status cue and model explanation, then the desktop navigation emphasis and mobile result access. The remaining items can be handled as copy and spacing refinements. No changes to the lifecycle architecture, design tokens, approved articles, authentication or deployment are needed for this review.
