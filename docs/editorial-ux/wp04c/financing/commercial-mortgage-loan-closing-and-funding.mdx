Closing converts a credit decision into signed obligations, collateral rights and a movement of money. These events can occur at different times. A borrower can sign in the morning while the closing agent holds the documents and funds in escrow until the release instructions are met.

## Reconcile the checklist to the most recent approval

Start with the final credit decision and agreed transaction terms. Track each item by evidence, responsible party, status, required date and sign-off authority. Counsel might clear entity authority, lien documents and title matters. Credit might clear a change in proceeds, guarantor support or material diligence. Operations might confirm verified wiring instructions, receipt of borrower funds and balancing of the settlement statement. Titles and approval roles vary by lender.

Use three distinct dispositions for open items. A condition precedent stops release until it is satisfied or formally changed by the authorized party. An approved deferral moves a specific requirement to an agreed date and records who follows up and what happens if it is missed. A recording or delivery item may remain outstanding under approved escrow or delivery arrangements, with the evidence and escalation path specified. In the shopping-center acquisition, a missing agreement for essential truck access may stop funding. The later return of a mortgage already submitted for recording can have a defined follow-up under the closing instructions.

## Compare the execution set with the approved deal

The note sets out the payment obligation. The mortgage or deed of trust creates the real-property security interest under the applicable law. The loan agreement or equivalent documents contain covenants, reserves, conditions and other terms. Assignments of leases and rents, personal-property security documents and financing statements, guaranties, environmental indemnities or cash-management agreements may also apply. Some functions can be combined in one instrument. Counsel identifies the steps to create, perfect or record the agreed interests and confirm priority.

Compare names, capacities and signing authority, then check amounts, dates, legal descriptions, lien position, recourse terms, reserve funding and reporting requirements across the executed set. For a refinance, confirm that the payoff statement is valid for the date of release and that the prior lien discharge can be obtained. On an acquisition, coordinate the deed, loan security instrument and title instructions so the documents support the approved vesting and priority.

## Follow the settlement money

The fictional shopping center has an $8 million purchase price. Assume $80,000 of other settlement costs and a $120,000 opening roof escrow, for $8.2 million of uses. The $5 million loan and $3.2 million of borrower equity fund those uses. Assume no deposits, prorations, payoffs or other credits. If the seller had an existing mortgage paid from the purchase proceeds, the seller's net and payoff would reconcile within the purchase-price disbursement. Adding that payoff again as a buyer use would overstate the cash required.

Before release, the parties agree the final statement and instructions, verify wire destinations through an independently verified contact and confirm receipt of the required equity. An authorized lender representative records funding approval once required credit, legal and operations clearances are in place. Depending on the escrow terms, the closing agent may receive money before permission to disburse. The applicable instructions control the sequence for releasing funds, recording documents and issuing title coverage. If a wire instruction changes, suspend the affected release and reverify through the agreed controls. After settlement, retain the signed statement, disbursement confirmations, recording evidence as available and reserve deposit confirmation. Reconcile what moved against what was authorized.

## Distinguish borrower funding from investor delivery

A portfolio lender can fund and hold the note. A Fannie Mae Multifamily lender must also meet the applicable committing and delivery procedures. Part IV, Chapter 4 of the Multifamily Guide addresses delivery, including alignment of documents and delivery data with the commitment. The June 2, 2026 guide edition cited here sets the reference point. Check the edition and exceptions applicable to the transaction. The advance to the borrower or settlement agent and acquisition or delivery into an investor channel are distinct events.
