The underwriter has sized a loan. Now someone with approval authority has to decide whether the lender should make it. That decision can turn on an assumption that barely moves the base-case debt service coverage ratio, such as whether an anchor tenant can cancel before maturity or whether the sponsor can cover a repair while another project is drawing on the same cash.

## Write the recommendation so another person can test it

A credit memorandum sets out the decision requested, the underwriting evidence and the terms on which the analyst recommends proceeding. Lead with the borrower and sponsor, collateral, loan purpose, amount, term, amortization, repayment sources and material departures from policy. Show where the numbers came from. A trailing operating statement measures a period of performance. A rent roll shows the lease position on a particular date. Link the two before giving credit to a recent lease, and state whether occupancy, rent commencement and collection are confirmed.

Put the proposed credit terms next to the risks they address. Shortening the term may bring a refinance forward into a period of weak leasing. A repair escrow can protect funds for a specific scope but does little for a recurring expense shortfall. Show the downside in dollars as well as ratios, and distinguish repayment during the term from repayment of the balance at maturity. A lender may also consider borrower exposure across other credits, concentration limits, internal risk rating and the authority needed to approve exceptions. The OCC handbook describes these controls in its bank-supervision context. Approval routes and memo formats vary by institution.

## Read the commercial decision in the numbers

Consider a fictional acquisition of a neighborhood shopping center for $8 million. On a proposed $5 million loan, assume $350,000 of annual scheduled principal and interest and $490,000 of underwritten annual cash flow after the replacement allowance. Coverage is 1.40×. An independent as-is appraisal of $7.8 million indicates about 64.1% loan-to-value. The contract price still belongs in the settlement budget. The appraisal provides the stated value basis for leverage.

Suppose the downside case reduces annual cash flow by $105,000 for a tenant departure, after allowing for expense savings. Coverage falls to 1.10× and the annual cushion after debt service shrinks from $140,000 to $35,000. Those figures exclude one-time tenant improvements and leasing commissions. The memo should show when leasing costs come due, who can fund them and whether they overlap a roof project. It should also test the balloon under a weaker value or higher takeout payment assumption. A borrower can make every scheduled payment and still face a refinance shortfall at maturity.

## Convert decisions into instructions someone can close

A decision may be an approval, conditional approval, return for more work or decline. Depending on the lender, an officer, delegated group or committee records the decision. Capture the approved amount and economics, the authority exercised, policy exceptions and their mitigants, and any items that call for a renewed decision. Distinguish an internal credit approval from an offer or commitment sent to a borrower. The lender's process and the terms of the communication govern that step.

In the shopping-center file, assume approval requires a $120,000 borrower-funded roof escrow at settlement, with the scope and release requirements in the loan agreement. Assign closing responsibility for the settled amount and servicing responsibility for administering later releases. If a revised bid changes the cost materially or a major tenant gives notice, reopen the affected assumptions with the authorized credit party. Signing off that a wire arrived cannot resolve a changed credit risk.

Write each condition with a deadline, responsible party, evidence and sign-off authority. A condition requiring confirmation of legal access belongs before funding if access is essential to the approval. Delivery of the recorded instrument may follow closing if the parties have an authorized recording arrangement and an assigned follow-up. An exception approves a departure from a requirement. A condition calls for action or evidence. An agreed deferral needs a new due date and formal authority.

## Identify who can approve the exception

A conventional portfolio lender generally applies its own lending and delegation policies within its applicable regulatory framework. For Fannie Mae Multifamily, the applicable lender contract and guide define delegated authority. A request outside that authority follows the applicable pre-review or waiver process. For a transaction intended for private-label securitization, the originator may need to meet intended purchaser or execution requirements in addition to its own credit process. Later inclusion in a pool is a separate step with its own governing arrangements.
