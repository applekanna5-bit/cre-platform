On the first day after funding, the servicer needs to know how much the borrower owes, where the money goes and what the borrower must deliver next. Loan boarding puts those terms into the servicing system and assigns responsibility for acting on them. Closing may finish with an orderly file and still leave a payment or covenant error if there is no check between the documents, the data and the opening balances.

## Build the working record from signed terms and settled cash

Use the executed loan documents for contractual fields and confirmed settlement records for opening cash and balances. Reconcile any loan sale or transfer balances to the relevant cutoff. An approved term sheet, credit memo or data tape helps identify what should be present. A discrepancy calls for resolution against the governing evidence before it becomes a billing instruction.

Capture the borrower, collateral and guarantor identifiers, effective and maturity dates, first due date, principal, rate and day-count basis, amortization and any interest-only period. For a floating-rate loan, enter the index, spread, reset and observation dates, applicable floor or cap provisions and any separate hedge monitoring. Record payment routing and application rules, late charges, prepayment terms, tax and insurance escrows, other reserves and account control arrangements as applicable. Keep multiple notes or companion interests distinct when relevant. Calendar financial reporting, repair deadlines, insurance renewals, cash-management tests and any guaranty release criteria, and assign someone to check each trigger.

### Test the first bill before it goes out

For the fictional shopping-center loan, the signed amortizing schedule calls for $350,000 a year in scheduled principal and interest, paid in twelve level monthly installments before rounding. That is approximately $29,166.67 per month. Assume the first bill is for a full scheduled period, the documents call for a separate $2,000 recurring tax escrow and there are no other billable items. The expected first bill is $31,166.67. A partial first period, different accrual date or separate interest collected at settlement would need to follow the signed terms and funding records.

The $120,000 roof escrow appears as an opening restricted balance reconciled to the settlement receipt. It does not reduce the $5 million opening principal and contributes nothing to the first bill in this example. If the system treats the roof deposit as a principal payment or bills it again as an installment, correct the boarding and verify the ledger and statement before mailing or posting the bill. Route repair invoices and release requests to the party with contractual authority.

## Manage custody and the remaining exceptions

Custody concerns possession, protection and tracking of required originals or authoritative electronic records under the applicable arrangements. A servicing file contains the working copies and records needed to administer the loan. Inventory the items each party receives, record their location and track acknowledgments. A note, endorsement, assignment or recording evidence can be required for delivery even if the servicing team already has a usable copy. For an outstanding recorded mortgage or final title policy, log the owner, deadline, interim evidence and escalation route. Confirm receipt and check the recorded or issued version before closing the item.

Where work transfers between institutions, agree on the effective date, payment routing, any cash in transit, and the contact for open requests. Send the borrower the payment and contact information required by the transaction and applicable rules. Reconcile the first payment and reserve postings after handoff. A successful data import does not show that cash reached the right account or that a reporting deadline generated a task.

## Identify authority after the loan leaves origination

A portfolio lender may service internally or outsource while specifying who can approve actions. Part V's servicing-file requirements and its loan document administration provisions provide the program reference points. Confirm the applicable versions and authority before acting on an amendment or release.
