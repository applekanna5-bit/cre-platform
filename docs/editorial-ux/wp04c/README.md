## Factual review

- The OCC's *Commercial Real Estate Lending* handbook, Version 2.0, anchors the discussions of approval, risk exceptions, underwriting, guarantor capacity, appraisal and credit administration. Its supervisory scope applies to OCC-supervised institutions. The memo format, condition assignments and boarding checks are teaching examples of controls that vary by lender.
- EPA's final rule recognizes ASTM E1527-21 effective February 13, 2023. The one-year and 180-day windows refer to federal All Appropriate Inquiries before acquisition. Verify lender reliance, scope and currency requirements separately.
- Fannie Mae Multifamily Guide Update 26-06 applies to surveys contracted on or after March 11, 2026. The Part IV §402.01 delivery reference is dated June 2, 2026. The live delegation, form and servicing references establish authority and documentation concepts. Freddie Mac Chapter 32 is cited to the April 21, 2026 bulletin edition. Confirm the applicable guide, contract and product provisions before publication.
- The BBCMS 2024-C24 agreement, effective February 1, 2024, provides a transaction-specific example of conveyance, mortgage files and servicing authority. The fictional loan runs as a portfolio example. Its amounts and workflows supply no agency eligibility thresholds or CMBS deal terms.

Independent editorial, credit, closing and servicing review remains pending. Review counsel-dependent passages against the intended U.S. audience and recheck live program guidance before publication.

## Worked example reconciliation

- Credit. $490,000 divided by $350,000 gives 1.40× coverage. After a $105,000 cash-flow reduction, $385,000 divided by $350,000 gives 1.10×. Base and stressed cushions are $140,000 and $35,000. $5 million divided by $7.8 million gives 64.1% LTV when rounded.
- Sponsor. $2.4 million less $500,000 pledged and $700,000 earmarked elsewhere leaves $1.2 million. Less $900,000 applied to closing equity leaves $300,000.
- Settlement. $8 million price plus $80,000 costs and $120,000 roof escrow gives $8.2 million of uses. $5 million debt plus $3.2 million equity funds those uses.
- Boarding. $350,000 divided by 12 rounds to $29,166.67 in monthly principal and interest. Add $2,000 in monthly tax escrow for the $31,166.67 first bill. The initial roof escrow is a separate $120,000 balance and opening principal is $5 million.
