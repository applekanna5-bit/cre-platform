## Illustration reconciliation

- Retail example. $520,000 plus $80,000 less $240,000 gives $360,000 annual NOI. After the illustrative $20,000 replacement allowance, the coverage numerator is $340,000. Less $250,000 in scheduled principal and interest leaves $90,000 on that analytical basis before other cash effects. $360,000 divided by 0.075 gives $4,800,000 under the stated hypothesis.
- Lifecycle example. The $180,000 repair escrow is a fictional condition funded by borrower equity. The example makes no claim that a release follows automatically.
- Transaction examples. $5.1 million less $5.0 million payoff and $100,000 uses leaves zero. $6.0 million less the same uses leaves $900,000. $5.0 million plus $750,000 gives $5.75 million combined principal.
- Screening example. $9.3 million uses less $6.0 million debt requires $3.3 million equity. $590,000 ÷ $480,000 rounds to 1.23×, $650,000 ÷ $480,000 to 1.35×, and $590,000 ÷ $510,000 to 1.16×. $6.0 million ÷ $9.0 million is 66.7% of contract price and $6.0 million ÷ $8.4 million is 71.4% illustrative loan-to-value.
