## Source and factual notes

- **Conventional Multifamily.** The existing OCC Handbook record supports property analysis, stabilized expenses and the OCC reserve convention. Its supervisory context applies to OCC-supervised institutions. Fannie Mae's DUS term sheet and Freddie Mac's borrower support page establish financing channels. The draft gives no agency rate, floor or tenant qualification rule.
- **MHC.** Fannie Mae Part III, Chapter 6 §603 is verified against the June 2, 2026 official PDF, with the collateral statement in §603.01B. Freddie Mac's Guide index linked Chapter 22 marked April 22, 2025, including site and home definitions, appraisal and title provisions. These are identified editions. A specific loan requires checks against current applicable provisions, including Fannie Mae §606, applicable tenant protections and any product overlay. The CFPB discusses home titling from the consumer side. EPA supports water and wastewater distinctions without establishing the permits or responsibilities of a specific community.
- **Affordable Housing.** 26 U.S.C. §42 supports the LIHTC periods, gross-rent and utility-allowance treatment. 26 CFR §1.42-5 supports the monitoring, certifications and recordkeeping discussion. HUD's April 2025 RAD comparison supports the PBV/PBRA contracting distinction only. Neither the RAD contract terms nor other program rules are imputed to the fictional example. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac product pages establish channels, and HUD identifies FHA multifamily insurance oversight. [Draft source records](source-records.json)
