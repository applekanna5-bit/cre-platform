# CRE Platform — Launch Content Registry

Status: M0.3 frozen candidate

## Purpose
This registry defines the canonical 48 launch assets for the Commercial Real Estate Knowledge, Underwriting, Financial Modeling & Decision Platform. It is the editorial source of truth for launch scope. Individual calculators are product routes and are not counted as editorial assets.

## Levels
- F — Foundation
- P — Practitioner
- I — Institutional

## Source classes
- A — Evergreen CRE knowledge
- B — Institutional/practitioner practice
- C — Program-specific authoritative material

## Update risk
- U1 — Low: evergreen concepts; periodic/annual review is generally sufficient.
- U2 — Moderate: lender landscape, underwriting practices and similar convention-sensitive material.
- U3 — High: program-specific requirements such as Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac; current official source and review metadata required.
- U4 — Current: rates, spreads, market statistics and news; explicit as-of dates and frequent review required.
